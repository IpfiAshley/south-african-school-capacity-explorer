Source / interpretation

Data & methods

This first South Africa release is designed for transparent, descriptive planning exploration.

Official source

Department of Basic Education EMIS, Schools Masterlist, Quarter 3 of 2025. The workbook includes school-level identifiers, province and district, learner and educator counts for 2025, quintile, urban/rural category, and coordinates.

This dashboard filters to schools marked OPEN, PUBLIC, and ORDINARY SCHOOL. It then keeps rows with positive learner and educator counts for ratio calculations.

DBE EMIS masterlist downloads ↗

What the ratio means

Weighted ratio: sum of learners divided by sum of educators across the selected schools.

Median school ratio: median of each school’s learners divided by educators, so a very large school does not dominate the statistic.

These are headcount comparisons, not class sizes, because educators may have different roles, subjects, schedules, and responsibilities.

Coverage & data quality

Unknown or missing school counts are excluded from ratio calculations, not silently treated as zero. Quintile categories not recorded as Q1–Q5 are shown as unknown.

Appropriate use

  • Use the aggregate patterns to frame questions for provincial and district education teams.
  • Review local conditions, staffing roles, school size, and data quality before planning support.
  • Do not treat this as a school ranking, funding formula, causal analysis, or measure of education quality.
  • No student-level information is included in the dashboard.
The school masterlist is a public DBE download. See its release notes and source documentation for data definitions and revisions. The app does not infer individual outcomes or school performance.
